# M33.4 final T480 health evidence contract

This is a capture contract for the final M33.4 proof bundle. It is not an
authorization to stage a release, release the maintenance hold, create Demo
order authority, force a trade, or close M33. It deliberately does not make
the existing M33.1/M33.2 receipts prove a later release.

## Bundle identity and integrity

Capture a new immutable directory under `runs/evidence/M33/` after the final
reviewed release is installed. Retain raw fixed-adapter envelopes and stdout
unchanged, plus a `SHA256SUMS` file and a manifest containing:

- final Git revision, governed configuration fingerprint, listener release ID,
  guardian/recovery release ID, and payload hashes;
- the T480 boot identity, account/profile identity hashes and the exact
  `GOMarketsMU-Demo` / AUD / EURUSD binding;
- each receipt filename, SHA-256, capture time, fixed operation ID and a
  redaction declaration; and
- `t16_connected: false` for every active-market observation, evidenced by the
  fixed local state rather than an operator assertion.

The final offline verifier must reject missing, changed, duplicate, stale,
unbound or malformed receipts. It must keep the existing
`FOREX_M33_LOCAL_TRADING_HEALTH_OK` marker scoped to the exact evidence it
validates; a new final marker must not be printed for partial proof.

## Required M33.3 recovery drill receipts

The controlled listener-fault drill remains under maintenance hold and uses no
order. It must retain, in order:

1. Fresh before-state listener, guardian, held-readiness and local process
   ownership receipts proving exactly one managed Session 0 client, zero
   positions, zero pending orders, no unresolved submission, current monitor,
   approved Demo identity and entry fence.
2. A bounded fault/action receipt identifying only the fixed listener task,
   request generation, boot, release and the no-order hold.
3. The executor request/attempt/receipt chain proving freshness, matching
   generation/state/action, `RUN_DEMO` desired intent, held no-order readiness,
   mutex ownership, budget consumption and no MT5/unrelated-client action.
4. Fresh after-state listener, guardian, process ownership and held-readiness
   receipts proving one managed client, restored monitoring, zero positions and
   pending orders, and `entry_eligible: false` while held.

Any uncertain process ownership, exposure, pending order, unresolved broker
submission, stale request, lost lock or failed monitor restoration is a
drill failure. The verifier must report intervention-required rather than
claiming recovery or attempting another action.

## M33.4 cold-boot and active-market soak receipts

Capture at least two cold boots after the final release. Each needs before and
after T480 boot IDs, no human sign-in evidence, scheduled listener/guardian
task identities, first successful local cycles, current Demo identity, local
audit availability and no-order held-readiness evidence. The two boots must be
distinct; old M33.1/M33.2 boot receipts cannot substitute for final-release
proof.

For the 24-hour active-market soak, retain bounded periodic local status
receipts with monotonic timestamps spanning at least 24 hours while T16 is
disconnected. The verifier must reject gaps longer than the declared sampling
bound, clock reversal, a changed release/fingerprint/boot, missing guardian
cycles, duplicate/unattributable clients, entry-fence loss, unresolved
monitoring, or a period outside the declared active-market window. A whole
24-hour duration alone is not evidence of useful runtime progress.

## Natural lifecycle to PostgreSQL P&L

This proof is pending until an eligible trade occurs naturally under separately
authorized Demo execution. It must never be manufactured by a forced trade.
When present, retain the listener decision/proposal, execution reservation and
broker response, open-position monitoring/exit deadline, broker closure and
reconciliation, and the fixed PostgreSQL lifecycle/P&L coverage receipts. The
offline verifier must require exact matching account, proposal, attempt,
position/ticket and broker lifecycle identities, `CLOSED_MATCHED`, AUD actual
broker P&L and the M33 coverage/P&L report row. A no-trade or ineligible trade
keeps this requirement explicitly pending; it is not a failure of runtime
health and cannot close M33-C8.

## Closeout sequence

The final verifier and an independent read-only review may recommend a human
decision only after all required receipts are current within the M33 24-hour
window. Chris alone may accept the recommendation, close the formal milestone
or approve any hold/entry authority change.
