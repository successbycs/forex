## Create your indicators and Expert Advisors

With the trading platform, you gain access to a whole ecosystem for
algorithmic traders. If you can't find the desired solution among existing
applications, you can create your own trading strategies and indicators. The
ecosystem provides advanced tools and services for developers of any level.
The extensive knowledge base and powerful development tools offer a swift
learning curve for beginners and assist professionals in reaching
qualitatively new levels.

The ecosystem offers:

- **MetaQuotes Language 5 (MQL5)** for developing technical indicators,
  trading robots, and utilities. This is an advanced, high-level language,
  closely resembling C++ in syntax, allowing you to write programs using
  object-oriented programming.
- **MetaEditor**, a professional editing tool offering intelligent code
  handling, debugging, and profiling options. The editor facilitates the
  development of trading applications of any complexity level.
- **Strategy Tester** with support for visual testing, optimization, genetic
  algorithms, and a distributed testing agent network, among other features.
  Simulate Expert Advisor operation on any price history segment and analyze
  comprehensive reports.
- **Subversion-based MQL5 Storage** with version control. Safely store your
  files, access them from anywhere in the world, and collaborate on large
  projects whenever necessary.
- The largest **free Code Base** featuring source codes of applications for
  the trading platform. Explore ready-made examples of Expert Advisors,
  technical indicators, scripts, and libraries, and use them as a base to
  create your own applications.
- A **unique knowledge base** — thousands of pages featuring detailed
  documentation and expert articles covering various aspects of programming.
- For professional developers, a specialized site to offer products and
  expertise to millions of users: **MQL5.community**, the largest community
  of algorithmic traders. Discuss ideas in the Forum, sell programs on the
  Market, or take on Freelance projects.
