## Predeclare the experiment

Before dependent execution, agree and record:

- Capital/reporting currency, all risk limits, trading windows, and expected
  operator involvement, using existing explicit approvals where applicable.
- Exact candidate version, parameters, selection/ownership policy, sizing,
  entry, SL/TP, exit logic, and rules for handling rejected or missing outcomes.
- Training/development and untouched chronological evaluation windows; retained
  data sources, availability assumptions, and conservative execution modelling.
- Treatment of bid/ask, historical fees/financing, slippage stress, and candles
  where stop and target ordering cannot be determined from retained data.
- Comparator and strategy-selection effects. A single primary candidate must
  not silently remove the contracted five-strategy observations or change which
  strategies can execute; propose an explicit amendment if needed.
- Forward observation end/review date, sample and market-condition coverage,
  uncertainty method, acceptance/rejection thresholds, and early safety stops.
- Recurring cost allocations, conversion basis, tax/development-cost treatment,
  drawdown limits, and operator-minute budget.

Choose sample/coverage and uncertainty criteria appropriate to the data rather
than a universal trade-count target. Do not peek repeatedly and stop at the
first profitable result. An approved observation window may end INCONCLUSIVE;
do not extend it or tune the candidate automatically until it passes.

## Collection without continuous AI consumption

Set up the approved ordinary Demo service to collect within its authority.
After verifying collection works, save a checkpoint with the exact observation
end/review condition and outstanding proof. Avoid repeated agent polling while
waiting for trades or market events; the operator can pause and later resume
the same goal. Keep protection active while AI work is paused.

On resumption, verify release/configuration continuity and inspect the new
evidence before analysis. A material rule change starts a separately labelled
experiment and invalidates affected proof; never blend versions to improve the
reported result.

## Completion and exclusions

An economic evaluation is complete when the predeclared observation/review
condition has been reached and a justified result is delivered. It can be
INCONCLUSIVE without implying a viable strategy. Missing required operational
proof remains PENDING and prevents claiming the full wave or milestone complete.

Recommend exactly one next direction: continue Demo observation under a new
approved window; revise/retire the candidate; or propose a separately authorised
production-readiness assessment. A supported Demo finding grants no live
authority and does not establish equivalent real-money fills or returns.

Do not add new agent systems, data platforms, portfolios, or live connectivity.
Use existing displays and ordinary reports. Do not expand the experiment to
manufacture a profitable finding.
