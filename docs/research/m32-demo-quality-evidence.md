# Evidence brief: final Demo MVP quality assessment

## Decision and hypothesis

Can the retained forward Demo evidence support a bounded, human-operated Demo
MVP handoff, with clear operating conditions and an explicit NOT_READY_FOR_LIVE
conclusion? This is an assessment of observed workflow capability, not a new
strategy hypothesis. Missing safety or explainability evidence must remain a
gap, not be replaced by a passing heartbeat or documentation.

## Context and evidence

EURUSD M1 on GOMarketsMU-Demo only. M30 retains the natural protected lifecycle
captured on 2026-09-22; M31 retains the predeclared 2026-09-23 01:15–01:25 UTC
refusal interval. Source paths are recorded by each proven milestone in
`project_state.json`; use their manifests and original bytes, not prose alone.
M31 was explicitly approved and proven at 2026-09-23T03:05:27Z.

Primary local evidence comprises those bundles, their independent reviews,
`docs/listener-operator-view.md`, `docs/workflows/m1-demo-decision-workflow.md`,
and the current fixed read-only dashboard collector. The registry defines the
M32 proof surface as retained forward evidence and a readiness assessment.

## Findings

Evidence-supported: M30 proves one natural protected and reconciled Demo
lifecycle, with operator-managed interactive terminal availability. M31 proves
a narrow no-trade workflow evaluation, not trading performance. Neither source
establishes continuous current uptime, an income target, Live-equivalent costs,
or safe ownership of unrelated manual positions.

Reasonable inference: a final handoff can reuse these facts without another
trade or timed observation. It needs a real read-only operator rendering and
clear instructions distinguishing current observation from retained proof.
Test this inference by capturing the established dashboard JSON once and
rendering it at normal/narrow widths. Missing sources must stay unavailable.

Rejected: a complete MVP implies profitable trading, Live approval, a minimum
trade count, no manual account exposure, or permission to reset/reforge risk
controls. The historical null baseline is not a qualified M1 strategy benchmark.

## Risks, recommendation and ExecPlan inputs

Selection bias, short-sample uncertainty, historical availability assumptions,
unqualified Live commission/slippage/financing, and absent direct manual/account
attribution remain explicit. Do not tune rules against this sample.

Create the M32 ExecPlan and an offline, reproducible quality assessment. Verify
parent artifact integrity and re-evaluate their raw facts with existing pure
validators; retain source/runtime/evaluator identities separately. Collect only
the established read-only operator view. Remove optional components from the
MVP-required operating checklist; do not delete/deploy runtime services without
an evidenced necessity and safe authority. Live readiness remains NOT_READY.
Require independent review, bound proof and human signoff before M32 closeout.
