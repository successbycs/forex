# M20.11 — controlled five-strategy Demo execution trial

## Purpose

M20.11 extends the existing M20 EURUSD/M1 Demo loop so that the strategy
selected by deterministic market-regime precedence may execute.  It is an
operator-owned, initially three-to-four-day learning trial—not a profitability
claim, portfolio system, or authority to trade a live account.  The operator
may extend the observation window when genuine selected signals have not yet
provided enough broker-reconciled outcomes; there is no arbitrary one-month
wait condition.

The trial remains limited to `GOMarketsMU-Demo`, `EURUSD`, and closed M1
candles.  It preserves one selected owner and one open EURUSD position.  A
strategy signal is never permission for five simultaneous trades.

## Controlled execution model

```text
fresh Demo quote + closed M1 candles
  -> common safety and cost gates
  -> deterministic regime precedence
  -> exactly one selected strategy owner, or NO_TRADE
  -> one persisted proposal before one bounded broker attempt
  -> owner-specific monitor / MT5 SL / MT5 TP
  -> immutable broker reconciliation and daily ledger row
```

The order is fixed: `UNSAFE_OR_UNTRADEABLE`, Compression Breakout, Trend
Pullback, Range Reversion, Session Breakout, Momentum Breakout, then
`NO_CLEAR_REGIME`.  An ambiguous selection fails closed to `NO_TRADE`.

## Deliverables

1. Every one of the five strategies has a deterministic, versioned entry,
   stop, target, cost gate, exit/time-stop, and explicit no-trade contract.
2. The strategy selected by the regime may be `SELECTED_EXECUTABLE`; all
   unselected strategies remain observable context only.
3. Existing Demo-only, cap, lease, proposal-first, idempotency, one-position,
   broker-side protection, append-only audit, and reconciliation safeguards
   remain unchanged or become stricter.
4. The existing dashboards clearly distinguish `EXECUTABLE`, `SIGNAL ONLY`,
   `BLOCKED`, and `NO SIGNAL`, with selected plan details in the listener and
   realised outcome details in the NZ-day ledger.
5. The two small append-only M20.11 migrations add immutable strategy
   selection/ownership and projected-cost components to the existing audit
   model. They preserve historical records unchanged; no separate analytics
   platform or parallel ledger is introduced.
6. Focused tests cover five-owner selection, one-position protection,
   counter-signal isolation, restart recovery, and Demo-only failure closure.

## Fixed M20.11 owner contracts

All entries use the executable MT5 side of the fresh quote: ask for a BUY and
bid for a SELL. Every plan is refused unless its projected post-cost target
return is at least the configured minimum. Every accepted trade has broker-side
SL/TP before monitoring begins.

| Owner | Entry / stop / target | Owner time stop | Invalidation exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Momentum Breakout | Two aligned candles outside the preceding five-candle range; stop at the opposite prior-range edge; 1.5R target. | 10 minutes | Two opposite closed M1 candles. |
| Compression Breakout | Confirmed break of the qualified compression range; stop beyond its opposite range edge; 1.5R target. | 8 minutes | Two opposite closed M1 candles. |
| Trend Pullback | Ordered trend, pullback and resumption; stop beyond pullback swing; 1.5R target. | 10 minutes | Two opposite closed M1 candles. |
| Range Reversion | Qualified rejection at a range edge; stop beyond rejected edge; range-midpoint target. | 6 minutes | Two opposite closed M1 candles. |
| Session Breakout | Confirmed liquid-session range break; stop on the other side of the pre-break range; 1.5R target. | 10 minutes | Two opposite closed M1 candles. |

The common +1R break-even movement is protective risk management, not a new
entry signal. A non-owner strategy signal remains context and cannot close a
trade.

## Trial evidence

The initial three-to-four-day trial records, separately for each strategy,
signals, selections, attempts, broker-matched opens/closes, actual costs,
realised AUD P&L, holding time, exit reason, regime/session, and rows that are
rejected, unreconciled, or excluded.  The operator may extend it if coverage
is insufficient. A strategy is never forced to trade merely to produce
evidence.

Use `./results` for the compact read-only scoreboard (or
`python3 scripts/m20_strategy_trial_dashboard.py`). It reports each owner's observed signals, precedence
selections, attempts, broker-opened orders, rejections, verified closes,
wins/losses, and realised net AUD P&L. A zero means no verified sample yet;
it is not a performance conclusion. `Not tried` separates regime selections
that did not reach MT5 from actual broker attempts.
The scoreboard filters to the exact active M20.11 v2 rule version, so prior
M20 experiments cannot be mistaken for current-trial evidence.

M20.11 is not complete merely when five labels display as executable.  It
requires fresh release-bound Demo evidence, one-position proof, intact audit
boundaries, and the current Triad-plus-domain recommendation required by the
milestone contract.
