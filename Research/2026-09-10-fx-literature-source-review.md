## Findings from the papers

**S1 — White (2000), Econometrica.** The Reality Check tests whether the best model encountered in a search outperforms a benchmark, accounting for the searched family under its statistical assumptions. A family-level result does not by itself identify a deployable individual winner. The architectural implication is an immutable trial register plus aligned candidate return series, including unsuccessful variants. Repeatedly inspecting a holdout still damages its role. [Full paper](https://www.ssc.wisc.edu/~bhansen/718/White2000.pdf); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=White+2000+A+Reality+Check+for+Data+Snooping).

**S2 — Neely and Weller, journal publication 2003; Federal Reserve working-paper version January 2001.** Their intraday rules did not show excess returns after realistic costs and trading-hour restrictions. This challenges the economics of rapid technical trading but does not prove every intraday rule impossible. Use it to demand cost-sensitive prospective evidence, not to reject all technical research. [Inspected working paper](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/docs/publications/frbsl_wp/1999-016.pdf); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Neely+Weller+2003+Intraday+Technical+Trading+Foreign+Exchange).

**S3 — Menkhoff, Sarno, Schmeling and Schrimpf (2012), Currency Momentum Strategies.** The BIS working-paper abstract reports cross-sectional winner–loser currency return spreads, partly explained by costs, and substantial obstacles to exploiting them. It motivates a diversified monthly research benchmark. It does not imply a comparable return on a leveraged retail account or justify an hourly alignment rule. The BIS abstract was inspected; no fresh replication was performed. [BIS source](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-366-currency-momentum-strategies); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Menkhoff+Sarno+Schmeling+Schrimpf+2012+Currency+Momentum+Strategies).

**S4 — Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen (2012), Time Series Momentum.** The paper finds return persistence over one to twelve months across 58 futures/forward instruments and studies a diversified strategy. Daily observations can implement a long-horizon rule; they do not make its prediction horizon one day. Test the published horizon and financing assumptions before adapting it. A single EURUSD daily adaptation remains a separate hypothesis. [Author-linked full paper](https://spinup-000d1a-wp-offload-media.s3.amazonaws.com/faculty/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/09/TimeSeriesMomentum.pdf); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Moskowitz+Ooi+Pedersen+2012+Time+Series+Momentum).

**S5 — Menkhoff et al. (2012), Carry Trades and Global Foreign Exchange Volatility.** Carry portfolio returns are related to volatility risk; high-interest currencies perform poorly during unexpected volatility increases. Carry is therefore a risk premium with downside exposure, not a natural fit for guaranteed small recurring income. A retail implementation must model actual broker long/short swap charges; the policy interest differential is not the cash credited to an MT5 account. [University full paper](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/3391/1/CTVOL_R3_v4_paper.pdf); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Menkhoff+2012+Carry+Trades+Global+Foreign+Exchange+Volatility).

**S6 — Ranaldo (2009), Segmentation and Time-of-Day Patterns in Foreign Exchange Markets.** The paper documents domestic-currency depreciation during local working hours and discusses liquidity/inventory explanations. This supports a session hypothesis, not a universal London–New York-only rule. Measure this broker's spread and execution distributions using daylight-saving-aware sessions. The earlier claim of a universal 10–100× off-session spread penalty is not established here. [Publisher article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426609001265); [SNB working-paper source](https://www.snb.ch/en/publications/research/working-papers/2007/working_paper_2007_03); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Ranaldo+2009+Segmentation+Time+of+Day+Foreign+Exchange).

**S7 — Coakley, Marzano and Nankervis (2016), How profitable are FX technical trading rules?** The study examines 113,148 rules across 22 currencies using daily data. Step-SPA substantially reduces significant findings, with different results across rule families. Crucially, accepted-manuscript section 2.3, printed page 6/PDF page 7, explicitly excludes transaction costs, although returns incorporate interest differentials. The abstract's attractive returns therefore cannot be described as net retail profits. This is a candidate-generation source, not an after-fee validation source. The abstract says 1996 while the data section specifies 1997 for most series; replication must follow actual data definitions. [University manuscript](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/16362/1/1-s2.0-S1057521916300400-main.pdf); [metadata](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/16362/); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Coakley+Marzano+Nankervis+2016+How+profitable+FX+technical+trading+rules).

**S8 — Hsu, Taylor and Wang (2016), Technical trading: Is it still beating the foreign exchange market?** Daily observations span up to 45 years and 30 currencies; more than 21,000 rules are examined with stepwise selection controls. The inspected manuscript reports positive results for some currencies/periods, includes costs estimated from forward spreads, and uses subsequent out-of-sample data. Section 7.1 examines break-even costs. This is meaningful counterevidence to a blanket dismissal of technical rules. Historical estimated costs and long samples still do not establish today's EURUSD retail execution edge. [HKU full paper](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/229640/1/Content.pdf); [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022199616300472); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Hsu+Taylor+Wang+2016+Technical+trading+still+beating+foreign+exchange).

**S9 — Bailey and López de Prado (2014), The Deflated Sharpe Ratio.** DSR adjusts Sharpe inference for selection and non-normality. It needs defensible inputs about the search and return distribution; a large number of correlated five-second assessments does not supply independent performance evidence. DSR is not the probability that an edge will persist, nor a replacement for temporal validation. [Author full paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Bailey+Lopez+de+Prado+2014+Deflated+Sharpe+Ratio).

**S10 — Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu (2017), The Probability of Backtest Overfitting.** PBO/CSCV assesses how often an in-sample winner ranks below the out-of-sample median across partitions. It is not simply the probability of losing money, and a low PBO can coexist with an economically bad candidate universe. CSCV is a selection diagnostic, not chronological future deployment evidence. Preserve the full return matrix now; defer this additional analysis until a meaningful candidate family and sufficient history exist. [Author full paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf); [university record](https://scholarworks.wmich.edu/math_pubs/42/); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Bailey+Borwein+Lopez+de+Prado+Zhu+Probability+Backtest+Overfitting).

**S11 — Jensen, Kelly, Malamud and Pedersen (2026), Machine Learning and the Implementable Efficient Frontier.** Published 15 March 2026, corrected/typeset 6 April. It integrates costs into the investment objective and reports economic feature importance. The inspected empirical application is NYSE equities, not retail FX. The transferable design lesson is to select models by implementable risk-adjusted net performance; the paper does not establish that this project's LLM or sentiment features add value. [Publisher full text](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/advance-article/doi/10.1093/rfs/hhag022/8524346); [Scholar lookup](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Machine+Learning+Implementable+Efficient+Frontier+Jensen+Kelly+Malamud+Pedersen).

**S12 — GO Markets account terms, current public page, not academic research.** The Mauritius entity page distinguishes Standard accounts with costs in spreads from GO Plus+ with per-side FX commission. This is enough to reject a universal zero-commission assumption; it does not identify the operator's actual account agreement. Confirm the exact account, currency conversions, commission schedule, swaps, minimum size and posted charges. Use observed statements and fills for calibration, not advertised minimum spreads. [Official account types](https://www.gomarkets.com/en/accounts-and-pricing/account-types); [official FAQ](https://www.gomarkets.com/en/faqs).

Hansen SPA is a reasonable alternative to White's Reality Check, especially when poor alternatives weaken the latter's power. Its original publisher page was inaccessible in this review; its role was cross-checked in S7/S8. Before adopting an implementation, inspect the original methodology and validate the chosen package, loss differential, resampling and interpretation. Implement one justified family-testing method initially, not every method named in a prompt.

## Resulting research priorities

1. **First establish measurement:** point-in-time data, equivalent replay/execution, all costs, matched risk and a complete search record.
2. **First conditional test:** one predeclared session/cost filter on the unchanged baseline, with an independent simulated account path and a prospective test.
3. **Next research challenger:** one slow daily technical or long-horizon trend rule selected in advance from a primary paper. Measure financing and turnover; do not transplant a reported Sharpe ratio. This can displace, rather than merely add to, the previously proposed H1 filter.
4. **Later diversification:** a small, explicitly specified liquid-currency research universe. Multi-currency execution needs currency-level exposure limits and separate authority. Five EURUSD strategies do not create five independent risk sources.
5. **Defer:** carry-first income harvesting, sentiment search across many cells, reinforcement learning and autonomous strategy optimisation. Each adds a substantial validation burden before its incremental value is known.

These priorities are **architectural inferences**. The session filter and slow challenger are **project hypotheses**. None is a claim that the platform currently has a profitable edge.
